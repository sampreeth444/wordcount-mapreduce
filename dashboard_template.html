A few words do most of the work

Plotted by rank on log scales, the counts fall close to a straight line. That is Zipf's law: the second most common word appears about half as often as the first, the third about a third as often. The dashed line shows that ideal.

What the combiner saved

The same job ran twice on the same input, once with a combiner and once without. Both runs produced identical output. The combiner adds up counts inside each mapper first, so far less data crosses the network to the reducers.
